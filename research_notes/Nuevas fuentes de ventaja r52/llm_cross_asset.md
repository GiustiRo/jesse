# LLM/AI news-text signals and cross-asset / macro-event signals for crypto and TradFi perps (1h-4h), as of Oct 2026

Scope note: about 16 search/fetch calls. Most crypto-specific sources were seen only as abstracts or search snippets. I read the full text only for Lopez-Lira and Tang (v6), and only its first ~100k characters. Anything flagged "snippet" should be checked before it carries weight.

## LLM return prediction from news, look-ahead/contamination, crypto sentiment OOS, and decay after 2023

### Takeaway
For US equities, there is real post-cutoff evidence that an LLM news signal predicts next-day drift. Lopez-Lira and Tang (GPT-4): Sharpe about 3 before costs, about 1.3 at 10 bps. That edge decays monotonically, from Sharpe 6.5 in 2021Q4 to 1.2 in 2024. It is concentrated in small caps and bad news, and it dies at 20 bps round-trip. Contamination is the dominant methodological risk: LLMs memorise pre-cutoff numbers, and masking does not fix it. Only post-cutoff tests or chronologically consistent models are credible. For crypto, I found no credible study showing an LLM/news/social signal that is profitable out-of-sample, net of costs, at 1h-4h. The published crypto evidence is in-sample fit, Granger causality, or effects lasting a few minutes.

### Cited Findings
- **Lopez-Lira and Tang ("Can ChatGPT forecast stock price movements?", v6 revised Oct 2025).** [arXiv 2304.07619v6](https://arxiv.org/html/2304.07619v6)
  - Design: uses gpt-4-0314 (training cutoff Sep 2021) on headlines from Oct 2021 to May 2024. The sample is 4,123 US stocks and 159,137 firm-headline-days, all post-cutoff.
  - Long-short drift, before costs: overnight news earns 0.34%/day (hit rate 58%, annualised Sharpe 2.97); intraday news earns 0.50%/day (Sharpe 2.63).
  - The "initial reaction" is untradable for most investors: hit rate about 93%, mean 3.06% overnight.
  - Drift begins about 15 minutes after the release. Returns in the 1-15 minute window are insignificant; from 15 minutes to the close the coefficient is 0.189 (t=5.12).
  - Costs (cumulative return Oct 2021 to May 2024, about 700% gross): >300% at 5 bps round-trip, >100% at 10 bps, unprofitable at 20 bps. Daily turnover is about 190%. At 10 bps, Sharpe is 1.29, or 1.34 with 25% partial rebalancing.
  - Decay: annualised Sharpe was 6.54 (2021Q4), 3.68 (2022), 2.33 (2023) and 1.22 (Jan-May 2024). The authors attribute this to LLM adoption, but suggestively, not as a formal test.
  - Where the edge sits: small-firm interaction coefficient 0.404 (t=4.75) against a base of 0.087. The short leg on negative news earns 26 bps/day (Sharpe 2.01); the long leg earns 8 bps/day (Sharpe 0.78).
  - Model ranking by drift Sharpe: GPT-4 2.97, OpenAI embeddings 2.53, GPT-3.5 1.66, MiniLM 1.42, DistilBART 1.26. Larger models help.
- **Glasserman and Lin (2023), "Assessing Look-Ahead Bias in Stock Return Predictions Generated by GPT Sentiment Analysis".** [arXiv 2309.17322](https://arxiv.org/abs/2309.17322)
  - They compare original headlines with anonymised ones.
  - In-sample, anonymised headlines perform better. This means a "distraction effect" (general firm knowledge biasing the score) outweighs look-ahead bias, especially for large firms.
  - Out-of-sample, look-ahead is not a concern, but distraction can remain.
- **Lopez-Lira, Tang and Zhu, "The Memorization Problem: Can We Trust LLMs' Economic Forecasts?" (2025, multiple revisions).** [arXiv 2504.14765](https://arxiv.org/html/2504.14765v2); [SSRN 5217505](https://papers.ssrn.com/abstract=5217505)
  - LLMs recall exact pre-cutoff values of macro indicators, headlines, stock returns and conference calls.
  - Instructing the model to respect dates does not stop recall, and neither does masking dates or entities: models reconstruct them from minimal context.
  - No recall is observed post-cutoff.
  - Implication: any backtest inside the training window cannot tell skill from recall.
- **He, Lv, Manela and Wu, "Chronologically Consistent Large Language Models" (ChronoBERT/ChronoGPT, v3 Jul 2025).** [arXiv 2502.21206](https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.21206)
  - The models are trained only on text available at each date.
  - For next-day stock returns from news, they reach Sharpe ratios comparable to a much larger Llama model.
  - They conclude that look-ahead bias is "modest" in that application, though it is model- and task-dependent. This gives a contamination-free way to build a historical backtest.
- **Crypto: ChatGPT-labelled Bitcoin headline sentiment (snippet only).** [Search result](https://econpapers.repec.org/paper/arxpapers/2510.08268.htm)
  - The sentiment fed AR, HAR, LASSO and SVR models of BTC returns and volatility.
  - It improved in-sample fit but produced no statistically significant out-of-sample forecasting gains.
  - Data frequency not confirmed.
- **Crypto: LLM-extracted buy/not-buy signals from news and influencer tweets (arXiv 2411.05577, Nov 2024).** [arXiv 2411.05577](https://arxiv.org/html/2411.05577v1)
  - Covers 9 coins, tested with VAR and Granger causality. Results are mixed across coins.
  - For the 3 best-covered coins, a 24h-aggregated signal Granger-caused price moves at lags of at least 6h.
  - This is a statistical-causality result, not a net-of-cost trading test.
- **Crypto: multi-agent LLM BTC trader (arXiv 2510.08068).** [Search result](https://econpapers.repec.org/paper/arxpapers/2510.08068.htm)
  - Backtest window Jul 2024 to Apr 2025, a very short window. The sentiment agent "did well in sideways markets".
  - OOS design and cost treatment were not confirmed from the snippet.
- **Crypto: tweets and BTC at the minute level (arXiv 2401.00603).** [arXiv 2401.00603](https://arxiv.org/abs/2401.00603v1)
  - Returns rise significantly mainly in the first 3 minutes after a tweet, and sentiment has no discernible effect.
  - A simple strategy at a 15-minute horizon showed gains that were not statistically significant.
- **Older Twitter sentiment studies.**
  - One study counts hourly bullish tweets, which predicted BTC returns and volatility over about 24h after controls; this is an in-sample regression. [Frontiers/arXiv listing](https://public-pages-files-2025.frontiersin.org/journals/physics/articles/10.3389/fphy.2019.00098/text)
  - A ZClassic hourly-tweet XGBoost study used 3.5 weeks of data on one micro-cap coin. [arXiv 1805.00558](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1805.00558)
  - KryptoOracle found that sentiment alone did not predict the next minute. [arXiv 2003.04967](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2003.04967)

### Inferences
- Even the best equity result needs costs at or below 10 bps round-trip. It lives in illiquid small caps and the short leg, and it had decayed to Sharpe about 1.2 gross by 2024. Crypto majors and Binance TradFi perps are the opposite case: liquid, heavily watched and news-saturated. Any news signal there probably resolves in minutes (the "initial reaction"), which is not capturable at 1h-4h.
- Two hard requirements for any LLM-text backtest in r52:
  - Use a model whose knowledge cutoff predates the whole test window, or a chronologically consistent model such as ChronoGPT.
  - Use news with point-in-time first-seen timestamps.
- Masking or anonymising does not fix contamination (Lopez-Lira, Tang and Zhu).
- A post-cutoff window for a current frontier model is short, months to about a year, so statistical power at 1h-4h will be weak.
- The equity drift starts about 15 minutes after release. If a crypto analogue exists, it is plausibly sub-hourly, and an hourly bar strategy would mostly miss it.

### Gaps
- I found no study of LLM/news signals for crypto at 1h-4h with post-cutoff OOS and transaction costs. This is either an evidence gap or a sign of negative results going unpublished.
- I did not get exact numbers on post-2024 decay of LLM news alpha; the latest Lopez-Lira and Tang data ends May 2024.
- I found no study of LLM parsing of central-bank communication (FOMC statements or minutes) traded intraday in crypto.

## Historical news datasets for crypto since 2020: availability, cost, timestamp precision

### Takeaway
GDELT 2.0 is free, has 15-minute granularity and "first-seen" timestamps, and is the most point-in-time-auditable broad source, but it is not crypto-specific. I could not verify CryptoPanic's official archive terms or price; third-party sources describe historical coverage as limited. I found no reliable public pricing for Kaiko's news product.

### Cited Findings
- **GDELT 2.0.**
  - Updates every 15 minutes and adds a mentions table; GDELT 1.0 is daily. Some 15-minute intervals are missing, so pipelines must detect gaps. [pypi gdelt](https://pypi.org/project/gdelt/)
  - The start date of the 2.0 data is reported as Feb 2015 by Aalto. [Aalto datahub](https://datahub.aalto.fi/en/data-sources/the-gdelt-database)
  - Files typically land 0-4, 15-19, 30-34 and 45-49 minutes past the hour. That post is about another GDELT dataset. [GDELT blog](https://blog.gdeltproject.org/web-part-of-speech-dataset-now-updating-every-minute/)
  - Web News NGrams are in BigQuery at 15-minute resolution back to 2019. [GDELT blog](https://blog.gdeltproject.org/web-news-ngram-datasets-web-ngram-now-available-in-bigquery/)
  - The DOC API `seen_date` field is the UTC first-seen time. This comes from an unofficial mirror and needs verification. [mirror](https://download.hermesedi.duckdns.org/.hermes/hermes-agent/optional-skills/research/osint-investigation/references/sources/gdelt.md)
- **CryptoPanic.** Needs an API key. A competitor's comparison table lists it at $29-299/month with 100-1000 requests/day and rates historical coverage as "Limited". This is a vendor claim, not official. [mcpservers listing](https://mcpservers.org/servers/nirholas/free-crypto-news); [roccomuso/cryptopanic](https://github.com/roccomuso/cryptopanic)
- **Newsdata.io.** Archive tiers start at about $199 for 6 months of history (third-party pricing page). [Capterra](https://www.capterra.com/p/266542/NewsDataio/pricing/)

### Inferences
- For a look-ahead-safe crypto news backtest since 2020, GDELT is the default. Filter it for crypto entities and use first-seen times plus a publication-lag buffer of at least 15 minutes.
- Scraped CryptoPanic or aggregator data risks timestamp drift: revised, edited or backfilled posts. Validate it against exchange price jumps before trusting minute-level times.

### Gaps
- CryptoPanic's official historical archive depth and price.
- Kaiko and other vendor news products: coverage, cost and timestamp latency. I found nothing reliable.
- Social-media history: X/Twitter API historical access costs. Not researched; I found nothing current.

## Macro event effects (FOMC, CPI, NFP) on crypto intraday, and post-2022 stability

### Takeaway
The strongest recent evidence is a March 2026 Federal Reserve Board paper (Kroner, Mohammed and Vega). Since about 2020-21, crypto reacts sharply and almost instantly to US macro surprises, like an equity-type risk asset: hawkish surprises push BTC and ETH down, and ETH moves more. Volatility, volume and spread effects last up to about 30 minutes. That describes a volatility and price-discovery event, not a multi-hour drift. Pre-2020 studies found little or no response, so the effect is regime-dependent.

### Cited Findings
- **Kroner, Mohammed and Vega (Fed Board, Mar 2026).** Seen via secondary repost of the abstract. [rwatimes summary](https://rwatimes.substack.com/p/how-do-cryptocurrencies-price-economic); [NY Fed staff report hit, possibly related](https://www.newyorkfed.org/medialibrary/media/research/staff_reports/sr1052.pdf)
  - Crypto shows sharp, near-instant reactions to FOMC, CPI and NFP, in volatility, volume and bid-ask spreads, lasting up to about 30 minutes.
  - Surprises tied to higher yields or risk-off push BTC and ETH down. Crypto behaves more like equities than like FX or commodities.
  - Sensitivity has risen since 2021, which the authors link to institutional participation. The NFP response flipped sign around 2020. ETH reacts more than BTC.
  - Order flow is more informative around announcements on Binance and OKX than on Coinbase.
- **Saadi et al., Annals of OR (2021/2023).** Uses 5-minute data for 2016-2019. Jumps are about 3 times more frequent in ETH than in BTC, and US releases matter more for jumps than German or Japanese ones. [IDEAS](https://ideas.repec.org/a/spr/annopr/v330y2023i1d10.1007_s10479-021-04353-0.html)
- **Earlier null results.**
  - A long-sample study (2000-2022, 30-minute windows) finds BTC "does not typically respond" to monetary or macro news, unlike other US assets. [run.unl.pt](https://run.unl.pt/server/api/core/bitstreams/a28b97c5-2eea-4448-96bb-db7526af22ed/content)
  - A Bath study finds no significant CPI or GDP effect on BTC returns, but does find effects for unemployment and durable goods. [Bath](https://researchportal.bath.ac.uk/en/publications/the-impact-of-macroeconomic-news-on-bitcoin-returns/)
- **FOMC volatility in crypto.**
  - An arXiv study measures the standard deviation of 5-minute returns from 12:00 to 16:00 on FOMC statement and minutes days against matched non-event days. [arXiv 2302.10252](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2302.10252)
  - Another uses hourly BTC data from Jan 2017 to Aug 2023 around FOMC decisions. [arXiv 2311.10739](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2311.10739)
  - A Thai study (5-minute data, GARCH, 2021-2023) finds BTC and ETH volatility fell over time and liquidity weakened during the hiking periods. [TCI-thaijo](https://so06.tci-thaijo.org/index.php/JSC/article/view/271735)
  - K33 (minutely FTX data) finds FOMC raises BTC volatility, especially after hikes began. [K33](https://k33.com/research/archive/articles/all-eyes-on-wednesdays-fomc)
- **CPI.**
  - Kaiko (via CoinDesk, secondary) reports larger BTC swings in the 6 hours around CPI releases. Per Kaiko in Jan 2023, CPI-window volatility was at record highs while average monthly volatility trended down. [DiarioBitcoin](https://www.diariobitcoin.com/bitcoin/bitcoin-mas-volatil-alrededor-de-datos-ipc-eeuu/)
  - Volmex (2024) finds CPI and Fed decisions raise BTC implied and realised volatility. [Volmex PDF](https://volmex.finance/Macroeconomic%20Announcements%20Fuel%20Volatility%20An%20In-Depth%20Analysis.pdf)
- **Post-FOMC reversals, from FX not crypto.** These mostly appear 12-24h after the announcement (snippet only, from the first FOMC search). I could not attribute it to a specific paper, so treat it as unverified.
- **Research gap.** A 2026 KSE thesis says few studies systematically measure BTC reactions to scheduled macro releases. It studies 2020-2025 volatility persistence after CPI and FOMC. [KSE thesis](https://kse.ua/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/illia-nazaruk_268722_assignsubmission_file_nazaruk_final_thesis.pdf)

### Inferences
- What is robust after 2020 is predictable volatility timing: scheduled 08:30 ET CPI and NFP releases, and the 14:00 ET FOMC decision plus press conference. Direction depends on the surprise, which needs consensus data available at release time.
- Practical, look-ahead-safe uses at 1h-4h:
  - Flatten or de-lever, or widen stops, into known event windows (a risk filter).
  - Condition on the sign of the surprise after release. The price impact is largely complete within about 30 minutes, so 1h bars would enter after the move.
- I found no evidence of a reliable 1h-4h post-event drift in crypto.
- TradFi perps on Binance (gold, silver, equities) should inherit their underlying's event sensitivities. Gold and indices react to CPI and FOMC as in the TradFi literature, but there is no perp-specific study.

### Gaps
- I could not access the full text and coefficients of Kroner, Mohammed and Vega: effect sizes in bps per 1 standard deviation of surprise, and drift beyond 30 minutes.
- I found no study testing whether a pre-FOMC drift exists in crypto.
- I found no net-of-cost tests of event-conditioned crypto strategies.

## Lead-lag: BTC to altcoins at 1h/4h, US equities to crypto, weekend/overnight effects, the US open

### Takeaway
At hourly frequency, BTC-ETH lead-lag is mostly bidirectional, and the authors judge it barely profitable. Practitioner analyses find persistent BTC-to-small-alt lags, but say they are mainly useful for execution or fast trading, not slower strategies. Crypto-equity linkage rose sharply after 2020: BTC-Nasdaq 30-day correlation reached about 0.8 in May 2022. Evidence on a US-open effect is mostly informal and dated to 2022.

### Cited Findings
- **BTC-ETH lead-lag (RIBAF 2019).** Uses hourly and daily data from Aug 2017 to Sep 2018. Causality is mostly two-way, and "intraday traders can barely profit" from hourly price discovery. [IDEAS](https://ideas.repec.org:443/a/eee/riibaf/v50y2019icp306-321.html)
- **BTC to altcoin transmission (Tokyo Tech).** Uses cross-correlation, Granger causality and VAR at high frequency. It finds delays in small-cap coins and tests lag-based trading. Results were not verified. [T2R2](https://t2r2.star.titech.ac.jp/rrws/file/CTT100941776/ATD100000413/)
- **Robotwealth (practitioner).** On hourly returns, some coins have persistent, predictable lead/lag relations with BTC. These are useful for execution or fast trading, probably not for slower strategies. [Robotwealth](https://robotwealth.com/topic-tag/data-analysis)
- **Bitcoin intraday time-series momentum (Financial Review 2022).** The first half-hour return predicts the last half-hour return, attributed to liquidity provision. [Birmingham](https://research.birmingham.ac.uk/en/publications/bitcoin-intraday-time-series-momentum/)
- **Intraday dynamics.** 5-minute patterns in BTC volume, volatility and liquidity. [Heriot-Watt](https://researchportal.hw.ac.uk/en/publications/the-intraday-dynamics-of-bitcoin/)
- **US session and NYSE hours.**
  - Quantpedia splits BTC returns into "intraday" (10:00-16:00) and "overnight". On NYSE-closed days the intraday part is stronger and steadier; on NYSE-open days the overnight return dominates. [Quantpedia](https://quantpedia.com/are-there-seasonal-intraday-or-overnight-anomalies-in-bitcoin/)
  - Bespoke (2022, via Advisor Perspectives): on weekdays BTC averaged about -1.5% during US market hours, with little effect on weekends. This is one firm's data from a bear year. [Advisor Perspectives](https://www.advisorperspectives.com/articles/2022/05/24/bitcoin-falls-when-the-us-market-opens-pointing-to-cash-raising)
  - Concretum reports intraday trend-following seasonality in BTC. [Concretum](https://concretumgroup.com/seasonality-in-bitcoin-intraday-trend-trading/)
- **Crypto-equity linkage.**
  - IMF (Jan 2022): after COVID, BTC volatility spillovers to the S&P 500 rose by about 12-16 pp and return spillovers by about 8-10 pp. BTC explains about 14-18% of equity volatility variation (daily VAR). [IMF GFS Note](https://www.imf.org/en/publications/global-financial-stability-notes/issues/2022/01/10/cryptic-connections-511776)
  - K33: BTC-Nasdaq 30-day correlation hit a record 0.8 in May 2022. [K33](https://k33.com/research/archive/articles/bitcoins-correlation-to-us-equities-grows-further)
  - Kaiko (Jan 2022, via press) reported BTC-Nasdaq 30-day correlation of about 0.58. Outlets label this inconsistently. [ai-cio](https://www.ai-cio.com/news/rising-crypto-equity-correlation-raises-bitcoin-stability-concerns/)

### Inferences
- A BTC-to-alt lag at 1h-4h is unlikely to survive costs for liquid alts. Where lags exist, they are sub-hourly and sit in illiquid small caps with high spreads and impact.
- Better use: BTC's 1h/4h return or regime as a conditioning filter for alt positions, rather than a standalone alpha.
- Equity-crypto contemporaneous correlation is high in risk-off regimes. That makes US-hours equity moves, and now Binance equity and index perps, informative about crypto concurrently, but there is no robust evidence of a tradable 1h-4h lead.
- The Fed paper's finding of informed order flow on Binance and OKX suggests price discovery during US releases happens on offshore perps first. That points away from "CME/equities lead crypto" at hourly horizons.
- Weekend and US-open effects come from 2022 bear-market samples or undated blogs. They need fresh replication on 2023-2026 data before use.

### Gaps
- I found no rigorous, cost-adjusted study of ES/NQ futures leading BTC at 1h-4h after 2022.
- I found no study of Binance TradFi perps' off-hours behaviour: weekend gaps against the underlying's reopening, or mark-price smoothing. The product only launched in Jan 2026.
- I did not verify the Tokyo Tech paper's profitability numbers or authorship.

## Cross-asset regime signals at daily granularity (liquidity, real yields, DXY), plus Binance TradFi perps context

### Takeaway
The claims that "global M2 drives BTC" mostly reflect shared trends. Same-month correlation of monthly changes is about 0.04, rising to about 0.5 at a 1-2 month lag, and even that rests on non-academic analysis. These are slow (monthly) regime variables, not 1h-4h signals. Binance TradFi perps launched only in January 2026, so any perp-specific history is under a year long.

### Cited Findings
- **Bit.com.** Level correlations are inflated by shared trends. The same-month correlation of monthly changes between BTC and global M2 is about 0.04; lagged correlation peaks at about 0.50 at 1 month and 0.49 at 2 months. It argues the cost of money (real rates) may matter more than its quantity in 2025-26. [Bit.com](https://www.bit.com/knowledge-hub/global-m2-decoupling)
- **Onramp (promotional).** Monthly 2014-2026 analysis of BTC against global M2 growth, DXY and 10-year real yields. It cites a 0.94 level correlation (May 2013 to Jul 2024) and an 83% 12-month direction match attributed to Lyn Alden. [Onramp](https://onrampbitcoin.com/research/bitcoins-macro-liquidity-cycle)
- **FX Macro Edge.** A broader global liquidity index beats M2 in explaining crypto returns; M2 misses volatility clusters. [Substack](https://fxmacroedge.substack.com/p/close-but-no-cigar)
- **Binance TradFi perps.**
  - Launched 8 Jan 2026 with XAUUSDT and XAGUSDT, USDT-settled, offered by ADGM-regulated Nest Exchange.
  - They trade 24/7 with the index fixed at the last in-hours value off-hours and a smoothed mark price.
  - [The Block](https://www.theblock.co/post/384743/binance-gold-silver-perpetual-contracts-usdt-settled); [TradeInformer](https://www.tradeinformer.com/broker-news/binance-launches-non-crypto-perpetual-futures-starting-with-gold-and-silver)
  - The line-up reportedly expanded to US stocks and ETFs by Apr 2026 (secondary). [Business Today](https://www.businesstoday.in/amp/impact-feature/story/how-binances-247-trading-hub-is-revolutionizing-crypto-stocks-gold-for-indian-investors-526422-2026-04-20)

### Inferences
- Liquidity, real-yield and DXY variables could serve at most as slow exposure or regime filters for a crypto sleeve, evaluated on monthly changes with publication lags. M2 is released with a lag of weeks, so it must be aligned to its release date.
- Every source with a positive claim is industry or promotional. Treat these as hypotheses for preregistered tests, not as evidence.
- For TradFi perps, the frozen off-hours index and smoothed mark mean weekend and overnight perp prices are partly "discovered" by perp traders against a stale index. That could create weekend-gap and reopen-convergence effects and funding-rate quirks, but this is untested.
- Backtests for these perps before 2026 must use the underlying (spot gold, equity futures), with a basis and funding model.

### Gaps
- I found no peer-reviewed study of DXY trend or real-yield regime filters improving crypto strategy Sharpe out-of-sample.
- I found no published data on Binance TradFi perp funding, basis or weekend behaviour.
